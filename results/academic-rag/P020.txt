Abstract—During the long-term operation of motors,
their working conditions are changing due to the industrial
demands or declining health status, and traditional diagno-
sis methods perform poorly in that case. This article pro-
poses a fault diagnosis method based on graph cardinal-
ity preserved attention network (GCPAT), which can work
under varying working conditions, and can be generalized
to the transient state. Diagnosis results are obtained by
analyzing signal-converting graphs, which are composed
of nodes and edges. First, the vibration signals are con-
verted into symmetrical snowﬂake images by symmetrized
dot pattern (SDP) method. Second, SLIC is developed to
make homogeneous super-pixels in SDP images as nodes,
and form graphs according to color, texture, and distance
features. Finally, the GCPAT is utilized to distinguish mo-
tor status. Compared with other state-of-art methods, the
results show the out-performance of GCPAT under varying
working conditions both in steady and transient state.
Index Terms—Fault diagnosis, graph neural network
(GNN), rotating motors, symmetrized dot pattern (SDP).

1) These methods only focus on the conditions that load or

3) These preprocessing approaches strongly depend on pro-

Recently, an intuitive signal representation method, the sym-

cation [11], [12]. Unlike these conventional methods, SDP can
directly transform original signals into mirrored symmetrical

patterns are directly related to the amplitude and frequency

been veriﬁed in [14], and a few researches have applied SDP

[15]. Gu combined the SDP and angular domain resampling
technology to realize diagnosis when speed is in transient state
[16]. Accordingly, the SDP method is effective and reliable for
fault diagnosis. Its application potential under varying working
conditions and transient state has been demonstrated and needs

Due to the SDP method, fault diagnosis tasks transform to
image classiﬁcation tasks, and CNNs have been widely utilized
in this ﬁeld [17]. Generally, CNNs process images in Euclidean
domain and only focus on the value in matrix. Composed by
nodes and edge connections, graphs provide an informative data
structure, which contains the information including values and
the connected relationships between the internal data. Recently,
graph neural networks (GNNs) have revealed its potential in the
fault diagnosis [18], [19]. In general, GNNs realize classiﬁcation
in nodes, edges, or graphs level. Zhang diagnosed the BF based
on node classiﬁcation [20]. In order to construct a graph, features
of each image are extracted and are aggregated into nodes.
Therefore, nodes represent samples and node label indicates the
fault type. But in this way, the overall features of image are
presented but the details are ignored.
In GNNs, graph attention networks (GATs) [21] have been
widely studied and applied. GATs show the superiority by as-
signing adaptive attention weights to different connected nodes.
Zhang [22] veriﬁed the ability of cardinality preservation to
improve the performance in the attention mechanism. Therefore,
a fault diagnosis method based on graph attention cardinality
preservation network (GACPT) is proposed, which can work
under varying working conditions. First, the raw vibration sig-
nals are transformed into the SDP images. Second, the simple
linear iterative cluster (SLIC) is developed to group pixels
adaptively into homogeneous super-pixels to form nodes, and
the edge connections are decided by nodes spatial distances.
Thereby, the detailed features in image can be noticed and fault
diagnosis is achievedingraphlevel insteadof nodelevel. Finally,
the experimental results for ﬁve motor statuses under varying
working conditions veriﬁed that the graph cardinality preserved
attention network (GCPAT) achieves the superior performance
compared with other baseline methods. The main contributions
of our article are concluded as follows.
1) TheSDPmethodisstudiedwithsignalsinsteadyandtran-
sient state. Due to the consistency of images, this article
reveals that SDP is an effective signal-to-image method
for fault diagnosis when load or speed is changing.
2) The proposed fault diagnosis method can analyze con-
structed graphs and realize accurately diagnosis under
varying working conditions.
The rest of this article is organized as follows. The theoretical
backgrounds of above methods are presented in Section II. A
detailed description of the proposed method is given in Sec-
tion III. In Section IV, experiment setup is explained and the
superiority of proposed method is veriﬁed by comparing with

article.

A. SDP Method

the kth and the (k + l) th values in the discrete time series,
this algorithm establishes the mapping relationship between
Cartesian coordinate and polar coordinate, presenting signals
in the form of symmetrical snowﬂake-shaped ﬁgures.

B. SLIC Method
The SLIC algorithm is an adaptation of k-means to group
pixels into expressively atomic regions, so-called super-pixels,
aiming to downgrade the complexity of image processing by
substituting the rigid pixel grid structure, as shown in Fig. 2.
By default, the only parameter needed to be set is: n, indicating
the desired number of divided super-pixels. First, image I with
N pixels is converted to CIELAB. It is a color space developed
by international commission on illumination, where u indicates
luminance, a and b are combined to represent color. Then, based
on position information x and y, cluster centers Ci = [ui, ai, bi,
xi, yi] are sampled on image I, which is divided into roughly
equally super-pixels with size S = (N/k)1/2. According to lowest
gradient position, the centers are shifted to seed locations to

C. GCPAT
GNNs analyze graphs by exchanging neighborhood informa-
tion recurrently to learn the embedding representation, so called
neighbor aggregation and graph pooling. As one type of GNNs,
GATs assign trainable weights in aggregation to distinguish
the information between a target node and its neighbor nodes.
However,thetraditionalGATsignorethecertaindistinctconnect
relationships of nodes, which limits the discriminative power of
the algorithm. Therefore, the cardinality information is needed
to improve aggregation [22].
Fig. 3 is utilized to explain the principle of cardinality preser-
vation. For source node u and u’, the edge connections are
different. In the original algorithm, regardless of the neighbor
structure of the node, the sum of attention weights of one node
remains at 1. However, in cardinality preservation algorithm,
the sum of attention weights is related to the number of edges,
so the neighbor relationship can be reﬂected. Therefore, the
summation function of attention weight is modiﬁed and the
cardinality information is utilized to update node representation
during aggregation.

III. FRAMEWORK OF THE PROPOSED FAULT DIAGNOSIS
METHOD
The overall framework of the proposed method is illustrated
in Fig. 4, including: signal-image-graph method and end-to-end
networks. The details would be described below.
A. Signal-Image-Graph Method
Step 1: Obtain the original time-series data. The radial vi-
bration signals are measured from motors with dif-
ferent status under varying speed and load working
conditions.
Step 2: Form visual distinguished SDP patterns. One dimen-
sional time-series are converted to snowﬂake images.
In the SDP method, because the rotation range d and
time lag coefﬁcient l will affect the image presentation,
based on the principle of maximizing image differ-
ence, images similarities are calculated as Fig. 5, and
parameters are chosen as δ = 30 and l = 4.
Step 3: SLIC is performed as an intermediate step from image
to graph. It adaptively divides an image into smaller
super-pixels. The only parameter needed to decide is
the desired number of super-pixels n. In our article, n
is set as 200 to ensure an affordable computation and
the details of the image can be described.
Step 4: Construct graphs based on super-pixels. The basic
information contained in a graph includes node fea-
tures and edge connections. First, the color and texture
features are treated as node features. The color features
arecomposedofl,a,andbinCIELABcolorspace,and
texture features include mean and standard deviation
value of angular second moment, entropy, contrast,
correlation, and inverse different moment. They are
ﬁve typical representative ﬁgures in gray-level co-
occurrence matrix. Hence, the node feature consists of
three color features and ten texture features. Then, The
spatial and color distance are combined to determine
the comprehensive distance between nodes [25]. A
node is connected to the ﬁve closest nodes in the
article.
B. Architecture of GCPAT
As listed in Table I, the architecture of the proposed network
contains two parts: GCPAT and multilayer perception (MLP).
After four convolutional-pooling blocks, outputs out1–out4 are
obtained, and the sum of them is utilized as the input of MLP,
which is used to produce the ﬁnal diagnosis results of motor
status. In the Table I, the number of nodes in all graphs is denoted
by nn. The number of node attributes is denoted by na. The
hidden layer size is represented by nh, which is set as 32. The
number of fault types is nf.

V. CONCLUSION
In this article, a fault diagnosis method based on GCPAT was
proposed for accurate fault diagnosis under steady and transient
condition. The proposed method was trained by steady state
data, which were graphs formed based on SDP method and SLIC
method. The results showed that the SDP images in steady and
transient state were similar. The SDP method was effective for
fault diagnosis when load or speed was changing. The analysis
of experiment results also revealed that the proposed method
achieved the strong diagnosis ability in steady state and superior
generalization ability in the transient state. The proposed method
suggested a promise for real-world industrial applications of
motor fault diagnosis.
